Live Season Survivor grading must defer strikes and eliminations until the authoritative full sport/week slate is complete. A non-terminal wipeout is decided against the entries alive before settlement; losses and established-slate forfeits void the week, while pushes do not. Terminal wipeouts close with the alive-at-start group as tied first-place winners using the shared prize splitter.

**Why:** Applying entry changes game-by-game destroys the week-start state and can leave a pool active with zero survivors. Calendar sports also cannot infer terminality from an empty next scoreboard response because feeds can be incomplete.

**How to apply:** Grade picks first, resolve postponed selections as pushes, settle once with a durable period marker, exclude voids from repair passes, and advance live non-NFL periods with a CAS after closure checks. NFL Week 18 and Super League Week 38 are explicit terminals. NHL/NBA require complete ESPN team schedules proving the last pickable period; partial or ambiguous data stays manual review. MLB keeps its separate revival semantics.
